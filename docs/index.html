Privacy Policy

Dublin Lions · club app

Last updated 27 September 2026

This policy explains how the Dublin Lions club app collects, uses and shares your information. It applies to the mobile app published as ie.dublinlions.app.

What we collect

Your name, email address, phone number (optional), member type, the names and dates of birth of any children you register, team assignments, event RSVPs, lotto entries, facility bookings and purchase records. If you set a profile photo, we store that image.

Why we collect it

To run the club: to communicate news and events, organise teams and fixtures, process payments for memberships, shop orders, match tickets, the club lotto and facility bookings, and to send notifications you have opted into.

Children's information

Parents and guardians may register children so the club can assign them to teams. A child's details are visible only to that child's parent or guardian and to club administrators. The app is intended for adults managing their own and their children's club membership; it is not directed at children.

Payments

Card details are handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, a device token is stored so the club can send you messages. You can revoke this at any time in your device settings.

Sharing and storage

Data is stored with Supabase (EU region) and payments are processed by Stripe. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

Your rights

You can update your profile at any time. You can delete your account and all associated data from within the app under Profile → Delete my account & data. You may also ask us to correct or erase your information, or request a copy of it, by contacting the club at the address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data, can be sent to manager@dublinlions.ie.
