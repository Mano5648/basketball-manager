How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Dublin Lions app and sign in.
  2. Go to More → My profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account & data.
  4. Confirm when asked. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

No longer have the app? Email manager@dublinlions.ie from the address you registered with, asking us to delete your account. We will confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile — name, email address, phone number, profile photo and member type
  • Your sign-in credentials
  • Any children you registered, including their names and dates of birth
  • Your team assignments
  • Your event RSVPs
  • Your membership records
  • Your club lotto entries
  • Your facility bookings
  • Your notification history and any push notification tokens for your devices

This cannot be undone.

What is kept, and for how long

Records of completed payments — shop orders, match tickets, memberships and lotto purchases — are kept after your account is deleted, because we are legally required to retain financial records. These receipts include the name and email address given at the time of purchase, together with the amount and date.

They are retained for as long as accounting and tax law requires (in Ireland this is generally six years from the end of the relevant financial year) and are then deleted. Your order history is also unlinked from your member account at the point of deletion, so it is no longer connected to a club profile.

Card details are never stored by the club. Payments are processed by Stripe, and Stripe holds that information under its own retention policy.
